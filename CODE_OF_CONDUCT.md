# Community conduct

Be kind, specific, and constructive. Discuss code and behavior rather than
people. Welcome different levels of experience, backgrounds, and viewpoints.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, and
publication of private information are not welcome. Maintainers may remove
content or restrict participation when necessary to keep contributors safe.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. Do not publish someone else's personal information in an issue.
